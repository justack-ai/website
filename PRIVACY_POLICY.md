## 1. Introduction

Justice Stack ("we," "us," or "our") operates RenterShield, an AI-powered lease review tool accessible at rentershield.justack.ai (the "Service"). RenterShield helps Canadian tenants identify potentially illegal, unenforceable, or high-risk clauses in their residential lease agreements.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with the care and transparency it deserves. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect your personal information when you use our Service.

Justice Stack is based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. This Privacy Policy complies with the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA), British Columbia's *Personal Information Protection Act* (BC PIPA), and other applicable Canadian privacy legislation.

By using RenterShield, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described herein, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information in connection with the Service:

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you choose to use social login, basic profile information provided by the authentication provider (such as your name and email address). Account authentication is managed through Supabase Auth.

- **Lease Content:** When you upload, paste, or otherwise submit a residential lease agreement for analysis, we collect the full text of that document. Lease documents may contain personal information, including your name, address, landlord's name and contact information, rental amounts, and other terms specific to your tenancy.

- **Payment Information:** If you purchase a paid plan or service, payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not directly collect, store, or process your credit card number, bank account details, or other financial payment instruments. Stripe collects this information directly and provides us only with a transaction identifier, the last four digits of your payment method, and confirmation of payment status. Please refer to [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for details on how Stripe handles your payment information.

- **Communications:** If you contact us for support, provide feedback, or otherwise communicate with us, we collect the content of those communications along with your contact information.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** We automatically collect information about how you interact with the Service, including pages viewed, features used, the date and time of your visits, and referring URLs.

- **Device and Browser Information:** We collect your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.

- **Log Data:** Our servers and hosting infrastructure automatically record information in server logs, including your IP address, request timestamps, URLs requested, and HTTP status codes.

### 2.3 Information from Third-Party Services

- **Authentication Providers:** If you sign in using a third-party authentication provider (such as Google or GitHub through Supabase Auth), we receive your name, email address, and profile identifier from that provider, in accordance with the permissions you grant during sign-in.

- **Account Management:** We use your account information to create and maintain your account, authenticate your identity, and provide you with access to your analysis history.

- **Technical Operations:** We use log data and device information to maintain the security and performance of the Service, diagnose technical issues, and prevent abuse.

### 3.3 Communications

- **Service Communications:** We use your email address to send transactional messages related to your account and use of the Service, such as account verification, analysis completion notifications, and important service updates.

- **Marketing Communications:** We will only send you marketing or promotional communications if you have expressly opted in to receive them. You may withdraw your consent at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in any such communication or by contacting us directly.

### 3.4 Legal and Compliance

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. We share your personal information only in the following circumstances and with the following categories of service providers:

### 4.1 AI Processing -- Anthropic

Your submitted lease text is transmitted to **Anthropic, PBC** (San Francisco, California, USA) via their Claude API for the purpose of AI-powered lease analysis. Anthropic processes the lease text to generate the analysis that forms the core of our Service. Anthropic's use of data submitted through their API is governed by their [API Terms of Service](https://www.anthropic.com/api-terms) and their [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy). Your account information, lease text, and analysis results are stored using **Supabase, Inc.** (San Francisco, California, USA), which provides our PostgreSQL database and authentication infrastructure. ### 4.3 Hosting -- Vercel

Our web application is hosted on **Vercel, Inc.** (San Francisco, California, USA). Vercel processes your requests and may collect IP addresses and other connection metadata in the course of delivering the Service. ### 4.4 Payment Processing -- Stripe

We do not have access to your full payment card details. Please refer to [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for details.

### 4.5 Legal Requirements

Justice Stack is based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. However, certain third-party service providers that we use to operate the Service are based in the **United States of America**. | Service Provider | Location | Data Transferred |

| Anthropic (Claude API) | United States | Lease text for AI analysis |

| Vercel | United States | Web requests, IP addresses, usage data |

**Important:** When your personal information is transferred to the United States, it becomes subject to the laws of that jurisdiction. In particular, personal information held in the United States may be accessible to U.S. law enforcement and national security authorities under U.S. law.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that our service providers maintain appropriate safeguards for the protection of your personal information, including through contractual obligations requiring them to protect your information in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Under PIPEDA and BC PIPA, we are required to inform you that your personal information may be available to foreign governments, their agencies, or their courts under lawful authority of that jurisdiction. By using the Service, you acknowledge and consent to the transfer of your personal information outside of Canada as described in this section.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

### 6.1 Retention Periods

We retain your personal information only for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law:

- **Account Information:** Retained for the duration of your active account, and for a reasonable period thereafter (up to 12 months) to facilitate account reactivation and comply with legal obligations.

- **Lease Text and Analysis Results:** Retained for the duration of your active account to allow you to access your analysis history. You may delete individual analyses at any time through the Service.

- **Payment Records:** Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 7 years as required by Canadian tax and financial record-keeping laws.

- **Usage and Log Data:** Retained for up to 12 months for security, analytics, and operational purposes, after which it is aggregated or deleted.

- **Communications:** Retained for as long as necessary to resolve the matter and for a reasonable period thereafter.

### 6.2 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time by contacting us at the address provided in Section 12 below. Upon receiving a verified deletion request, we will:

1. Delete your account and authentication credentials;
2. Delete your stored lease text and analysis results;
3. Delete or de-identify your usage data;
4. Retain only such information as is required by law or necessary for legitimate legal purposes (such as financial records required for tax compliance).

We will complete your deletion request within **30 days** of receipt, unless a longer period is required by law. We will confirm deletion in writing to the email address associated with your account.

Please note that we cannot retroactively delete data that has already been transmitted to and processed by third-party service providers (such as Anthropic for AI analysis). However, Anthropic's API data retention policies govern the deletion of data on their systems.

## 7. Security Measures

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss. These measures include:

- **Encryption in Transit:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security) / HTTPS.

- **Encryption at Rest:** Data stored in our database infrastructure is encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption protocols.

- **Authentication Security:** User authentication is managed through Supabase Auth, which implements secure password hashing, session management, and optional multi-factor authentication.

- **Access Controls:** Access to personal information within our systems is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

- **API Security:** Communications with third-party APIs (including Anthropic) are authenticated and encrypted.

- **Infrastructure Security:** Our hosting provider (Vercel) and database provider (Supabase) maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance and implement comprehensive security programs.

- **Regular Review:** We periodically review our security practices and update them as necessary to address emerging threats and vulnerabilities.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.

If you become aware of any unauthorized access to your account or personal information, please contact us immediately at the address provided in Section 12.

- **Right to Correction:** You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information we hold about you. If we have disclosed the inaccurate information to a third party, we will notify that third party of the correction where practicable.

- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You have the right to withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. - **Right to Complain:** You have the right to file a complaint with the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** or the **Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia** if you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with applicable privacy legislation.
