# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Justack.ai ("the Platform") is an open-source legal information platform operated by Humilitas Group Limited, a corporation incorporated in Ontario, Canada ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** If you create an account, we may collect your name and email address.
- **User Inputs:** When you use our tools, you may enter legal questions or factual information. This information is processed during your session and is not saved to any user profile or stored by the Platform after your session ends.
- **Payment Information:** If you make a payment, your payment information is processed by Stripe. We do not store your payment card details.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** We may collect information about how you use the Platform, including pages visited, features used, and session duration.
- **Device Information:** We may collect information about your device, including browser type, operating system, and IP address.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

(a) provide, maintain, and improve the Platform;
(b) process transactions;
(c) respond to your inquiries;
(d) comply with legal obligations; and
(e) protect the security of the Platform.

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party service providers to operate the Platform:

- **Vercel** — Hosting and deployment.
- **Supabase** — Database and authentication.
- **Stripe** — Payment processing.
- **Sanity** — Content management.
- **AI Model Provider** — User inputs submitted to Platform tools are transmitted to a third-party AI model provider for processing. The current provider is Anthropic (Claude). We reserve the right to change AI model providers with notice to all users. No AI model provider used by the Platform is permitted to use your inputs for model training.

Each provider processes your information only as necessary to provide their services and in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## 5. Your Legal Information

When you enter details into our tools, that information is processed during your session only. It is transmitted to our AI model provider for processing and a response is returned to you. Your legal information is not saved to any user profile or stored by the Platform after your session ends.

## 6. Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Canada, including the United States, by our third-party service providers. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Platform. We retain usage data in anonymized or aggregated form for analytics purposes. User inputs are not retained after your session ends.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

(a) access the personal information we hold about you;
(b) request correction of inaccurate personal information;
(c) request deletion of your personal information;
(d) data portability (where required by applicable law);
(e) withdraw consent to processing of your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@justack.ai.

## 9. Quebec Residents

If you reside in Quebec, the following applies in addition to the rest of this Privacy Policy.

Your personal information may be transferred and processed outside Quebec, including in the United States, by our AI model provider. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

We are committed to conducting a privacy impact assessment where required by Quebec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (Law 25) in connection with any cross-border transfer of personal information.

## 10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 12. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first at hello@justack.ai.

You may also file a complaint with:

- **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC)** — priv.gc.ca
- **Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (OIPC)** — oipc.bc.ca
- **Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI)** — cai.gouv.qc.ca

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify users by posting the updated policy on the Platform with a revised "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Platform after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

## 14. Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA), the *Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act* (British Columbia), Quebec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (Law 25), and other applicable Canadian federal and provincial privacy legislation.
